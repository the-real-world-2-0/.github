# The Real World 2.0

The Real World 2.0 is the successor to Andrew Tate’s online education platform, The Real World (also known as The Real World 1.0). It continues to offer courses in online wealth‑creation fields such as copywriting, cryptocurrency, e‑commerce, and AI automation. It also introduces an expanded productivity ecosystem with AI tools, resume‑ready certifications, a job board, and a built‑in digital wallet.
