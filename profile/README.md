# The Real World 2.0

The Real World 2.0 is Andrew Tate’s upgraded online education platform. It teaches modern wealth creation methods such as copywriting, cryptocurrency, e-commerce, AI automation, and more.

Unlike the previous version, The Real World (also known as The Real World 1.0), it offers upcoming features such as a full productivity ecosystem with job boards, AI tools, resume-ready certifications, and a built-in wallet.
