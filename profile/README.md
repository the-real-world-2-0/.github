# The Real World 2.0

The Real World 2.0 is the successor to The Real World, an online education platform focused on wealth creation. It continues to offer courses in online wealth‑creation fields such as strategic marketing, cryptocurrency, e‑commerce, and AI automation. It also introduces an expanded productivity ecosystem with AI tools, resume‑ready certifications, a job board, and a built‑in digital wallet.
